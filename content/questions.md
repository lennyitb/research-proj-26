
## A Code of Ethics

During the time Phreaking was a thing, the Bell Telephone company was considered a "regulated monopoly", but they never dared use their sweeping powers to the full extent that would have been permissible out of fear of losing their status. This is called self-regulating behavior, and the phreaking community was known to engage in just the same thing. Their hobby was illegal technically, but for a time, the phone company tacitly permitted it, because they weren't too much of a pain.

In the end, both the community and its overlord are considered to have "overstepped their bounds", and in both cases, a crackdown ensued. The infamous, omnipotent "Ma Bell" was broken up, and a good chunk of the phreakers came to face serious legal consequences. 

While Draper and Engressia were bent on recruiting as many others into phreaking as possible, other phreakers dissented to this. They were eventually proven right, but did they accomplish anything, or did they stand in the way of progress, inclusivity, and enlightenment only to get run over in the end?

Read "Hiding in the Walls" for most of the context on how to address this.
