Schedule and Format for Research Project Presentation
When will the projects be presented?

The projects should be up and running on Monday, May 4. During the last week of class and the final exam period, visit each other's projects and participate in the discussions. You will still be expected to participate in discussions on the same schedule as the rest of the course. In addition, project hosts should moderate your discussions to keep the discussion productive.

What the project needs to include

The research project can take a variety of forms, but there are some elements that must be included.

1. Identify the topic of your project.

2. Present a description of the particular Internet group or activity that you are discussing.

3. Provide some analysis of the cultural characteristics of the group or activity. Don’t get too worried about what this means; it mainly means identifying characteristic behavior and interactions among the participants.

4. Discuss how the on-line nature of the group or activity affects its cultural characteristics. If the group or activity is also characterized by in-person interactions, it would be a good idea to discuss how the in-person characteristics differ from the on-line characteristics.

With that in mind, here are some possible formats:

·        A narrative (like a research paper) with links and references included so readers can access your research material.

·        A detailed outline with links to further discussion, illustrated again with links and references.

·        A narrative or outline with these features plus embedded videos, applications.

·        A narrative or outline with these features plus interactive features (i.e., participation by readers).

There are doubtless many other possibilities. Depending on your facility with constructing home pages,  you can turn this into a real multimedia experience.

You will also need to moderate a forum where your project can be discussed by the members of the class. That means you'll need to create a discussion or a set of discussions. For instructions on how to create a discussion, see below.
